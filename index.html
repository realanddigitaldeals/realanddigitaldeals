For Banks & Credit UnionsLicense the cashflow projection format

Your banking app shows a balance. It doesn't show a future. Count On It's projection engine projects each member's balance day by day — bills, paychecks, and debt payments included — before they overdraft.

Platform vendors don't ship this. Building it in-house takes months of development. We license the format: your members see their projected balance inside your brand, overdraft fees fall, and your institution differentiates from every big bank in town.
